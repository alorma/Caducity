Stop throwing away food and money. Caducity shows you what is about to expire, so you eat it in time.

WHAT EXPIRES, AT A GLANCE
Home groups your products as expired, today, tomorrow and the rest of the week, most urgent first. Each product has a status: fresh, expiring soon, expired or frozen.

ADD A PRODUCT IN A FEW TAPS
Type the name, pick a category and type the date with the number keyboard, just as it is printed on the package. Adding several units creates one independent item for each, and you can add many products in a row.

ONE TAP TO ACT
Open any item to consume it, freeze it, change its date, edit it, move it or delete it. Made a mistake? Undo it.

YOUR PANTRY, BY CATEGORY
Every category has its own shape, icon and a bar with the state of its products. Filter by status and by day with the 7-day strip.

CALENDAR
See what expires each day of the month, with a colored dot for each status.

"EXPIRING SOON" YOUR WAY
Milk and canned food do not need the same notice. Set how many days ahead each category counts as expiring soon.

ONE REMINDER A DAY
A single daily notification, at the time you choose, with what expires today and tomorrow.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your products stay on this device. No account, no sign-up. Anonymous usage statistics are off by default and you can change them in Settings. Back up and restore your data from Settings.

Available in English, Catalan and Spanish, with light and dark themes.

Caducity helps you plan, but it does not replace your judgment: always check the product and its package before eating it.
